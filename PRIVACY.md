# Privacy Policy for Proot Distro

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026  
**Application:** Proot Distro (`ng.kiri.prootdistro`)  
**Developer:** Kiri Research Labs (`https://kiri.ng`)  
**Contact:** `contact@nwokike.ng`

---

## 1. Overview

Proot Distro is a developer utility that runs user-requested, rootless Linux containers on Android using PRoot and `proot-distro`. We respect your privacy and believe that developer tools should operate locally and transparently.

---

## 2. Information We Handle

### A. Personal Data
Proot Distro **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information**. The application requires no account creation, registration, or login.

### B. Device Storage & Local Files
All container filesystems, configuration files, terminal scrollback, and package caches reside exclusively in the application's private storage sandbox on your device.
- Container files, scripts, and commands remain strictly on your device.
- No user-created files or container data are transmitted to any first-party or third-party server.
- Container data can be completely removed at any time through the container management interface.

### C. Network Communications

Proot Distro makes network requests under two distinct scenarios:

1. **User-Initiated Container Downloads:**  
   When you choose to install or search for a container, the app communicates directly with public container registries (such as Docker Hub) to retrieve public container image layers and metadata. Optional Docker Hub authentication tokens entered in Settings remain stored locally on your device.

2. **Advertising (Google AdMob):**  
   The application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support development. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction metrics subject to Google's Privacy Policy:  
   https://policies.google.com/privacy  
   In regulated regions (including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), consent is requested and managed via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) at application launch.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. Network interactions are limited to the container registries you connect to and Google AdMob as described above.

---

## 4. Open Source & Source Code Availability

Proot Distro wraps open-source components including PRoot and `proot-distro`. In compliance with open-source licenses, full license texts and source code access are available in the application under **Settings → About → Open-source licenses** and in the companion [THIRD-PARTY-SOURCES.md](THIRD-PARTY-SOURCES.md) file.
